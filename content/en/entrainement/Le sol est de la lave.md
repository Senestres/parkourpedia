## Objective
Practice jumping and/or climbing depending on the obstacles used

Tackling challenges

## Organisation
⌛Duration: 10min

👥Participants: individual

⚙️️Equipment: various obstacles (walls, trees, rails, benches, small mats, ropes...)

## Description
The participants must move from point A to point B without touching the ground.

## Variations
➖/➕Participants have "lives". Every time they step into the lava, they lose a life.

➖Participants can help each other to stay balance, overcome obstacles, etc.

➕Every time a player passes through, a hold or obstacle becomes unusable or is removed from the game.

➕On the second try, participants lose a member (moving around with one hand or one foot).
