## Objective
Create, memorise and reproduce a sequence of movements.

## Organisation
⌛Duration: 10+ min

👥Participants: groups of 2-5

⚙️️Equipment: various obstacles
## Description
The first player makes the parkour move of his choice. The next reproduces that moves and adds one. And so forth, each reproducing the sequence of moves and adding +1 move.

Anyone who makes a mistake is eliminated (they may continue to play, but can no longer add any moves). When all players are eliminated, start again with a new line.

## Variations
➖/➕Gives players multiple lives.

➖Every time a move is added, all participants do the whole sequence before a new move is added.

➕Each technique can only be used once.

## Notes
Practitioners should be comfortable with 3-4 different techniques before this game makes sense. But this can happen very quickly: they can import moves from other disciplines (a cartwheel,a breakdancing move...) and they can use the same technique on different obstacles.

To really work on flow: after a certain number of moves, or when there is a mistake, "freeze" the line and repeat it until it is entirely seamless.
