## Objective
Warm-up

## Organisation
⌛Duration: 5min

👥Participants: works regardless of the number of participants

⚙️️Equipment: none (indoor space with lines on the floor or adapt the instructions to the environment)

## Description
The participants move freely in a given space. When the leader says:

- a number: the participants adapt their speed (0=statue; 10=sprint)
- a colour: the participants do a precision jump on a line of the indicated colour

Outdoors, it is possible to use colors types of surfaces (metal, wood, concrete, etc.) or types of obstacles (walls, stairs...)

## Variations
➕When they don't stick the landing, they carry out a forfeit (do 5 squats...)
