## Objective
Cooperate with teammates to get from point A to point B
Respect your limits and those of others.

## Organisation
⌛Duration: 15-20 min

👥Participants: 3 - 12

⚙️Equipment: various obstacles, boxes, mats

## Description
Prepare the game area with islands to be able to move from point A to point B. When a participant falls into lava, everyone starts again at point A. Participants can help each other overcome obstacles.
Change the terrain after each victory to make the level more and more difficult.

## Variations
➖/➕Vary the types of obstacles, the distances and heights.

➕Add constraints: compulsory or prohibited moves, compulsory checkpoints, etc.

➕➕ Add time constraints.

## Notes
The goal is to get participants to think about the collective victory rather than personal glory. They should not attempt a jump that they are not 100% sure of succeeding or take unnecessary risks.
