## Objective
Warm-up and prepare movements similar to vaults

## Organisation
⌛Duration: 5-10min

👥Participants: group

⚙️️Equipment: none

## Description
In a restricted space, the humans must escape monster that move around in different ways (wolves on all fours, chest to the ground; spiders on all fours with their back to the ground, etc.) When a humans gets caught, they become the type of monster that touched them.

## Variations
➖/➕All participants are monsters that move around on all fours. They must touch the hand (or shoulders) of the others without getting touched themselves. Those who are caught become a monster of the species that touched them. This can be played in 1v1 or free-for-all.

## Notes
This game is extremely intense, so plan on playing short rounds, even if that means playing several rounds with breaks in between.
