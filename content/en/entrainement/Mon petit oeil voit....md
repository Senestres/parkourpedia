## Objective
Read the movement possibilities of the environment

## Organisation
⌛Duration: 10min

👥Participants: groups of 3-7 participants

⚙️️Equipment: various obstacles

## Description
A participant announces a move to be performed, for example: "I spy with my little eye... a tic-tac !" The other players must perform the move as fast as possible and come back to the starting zone. The last to arrive becomes the announcer for the next move.

## Variations
➖Instead of the name of a move, the announcer says the name of an obstacle or a type of surface. The others may use any parkour technique on this obstacle or surface.

➕The announcer gives a sequences of moves (2-5 moves), for example "my little eye sees... a tic-tac precision to cat leap !"

## Notes
The participants must know the names of at least five or six techniques to make this game playable.
