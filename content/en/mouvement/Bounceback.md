## Description
A bounceback consists of pushing oneself away from an obstacle with both feet, generally after a jump that is too short to bring the body above the obstacle.

## Notes
Getting comfortable with bouncebacks allows oneself to attempt jumps at the limit of one's physical and mental abilities without putting oneself in danger.
