## Description
Raiz (rotation on the longitudinal axis) with the support of the back on a rail, before taking contact with one hand on the ground, similar to a scoot.

## Notes
To perform this effectively, the bar should be placed diagonally across the back, from one shoulder to the opposite hip, so that the hand can quickly reach the ground.
