## Description
Vault with a half turn over an obstacle to land in a hanging position on the other side.

## Notes
This vault is made for height situations. One hand should always stay in contact with the obstacle, which implies that one hand should be in supination at the beginning of the movement.
This move can be learned by turning from a safety vault position.
