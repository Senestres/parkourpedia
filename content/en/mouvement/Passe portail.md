## Description
One hand is place on the top of the obstacle, the other on the vertical surface on the opposite side of the obstacle, in order to rotate and get the legs over the obstacle.

## Notes
This vault is very useful for high obstacles, because it allows to keep the center of gravity below the top of the obstacle before letting go.
