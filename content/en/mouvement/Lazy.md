## Description
Vault starting with support from the hand closer to the wall. The legs are thrown in front with a scissor motion. Once the legs are over the obstacle, the second hand contacts the obstacle to push off.

## Notes
There is no consensus on the use of the legs: is a lazy done with the inside leg first (like a scissors jump in athletics), or outside leg (similar to a speed vault) ?
