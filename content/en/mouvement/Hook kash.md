## Description
A vault where one leg is passed over a bar to hook it under the knee, in order to support oneself with the leg and both hands. The other leg threads through the space between the hands, similar to a kash.

## Notes
This can be used as a progression to learn the kash on a bar, especially for learners who feel like they can't get their legs through their hands.
