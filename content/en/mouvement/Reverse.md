## Description
Vault with a full rotation on the longitudinal axis during flight. Contrary to the butterfly vault, the rotation involves turning one's back to the obstacle.

## Notes
The vault can be used on the floor (lateral quadrupedal movement with half turns)

A good progression is to do the lateral vault with a delayed rotation, to prevent the fear of rotating over the obstacle.
