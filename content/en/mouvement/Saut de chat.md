## Description
Vault involving leaping forward over an obstacle and pushing off with both hands simultaneously to bring the body in a more upright position. Unlike some other vaults, there is no lateral tilt, and the body remains leaning forward at all times.

## Notes
Using a piece of string between two boxes or obstacles can help learning the move without the fear of the feet getting caught at the top of the obstacle.
