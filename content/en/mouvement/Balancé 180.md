## Description
In hanging position from a bar, rotate 180° on the longitudinal axis after swinging forward.

## Notes
The technique will vary depending on the amplitude of the swing: if it is sufficient, it is a matter of releasing the bar at the top of the swing. When the amplitude is insufficient, it is not possible to release the bar with both hands simultaneously.
