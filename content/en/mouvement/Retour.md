## Description
Rotation of 180° on the longitudinal axis after pushing off with a step on a vertical surface, to get away from the wall. Generally, a pull-up motion of the arms accompanies the movement to gain distance and/or height.

## Notes
Alternated footwork on the wall allow to get the center of gravity higher before pushing off, and therefore allowing to gain distance or to project oneself towards a higher obstacle.
